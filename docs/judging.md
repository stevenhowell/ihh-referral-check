# Judging

## How it runs

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 4:00 PM | Submissions close. Judges review every submission and vote for the demos they want to see |
| 4:30 to 5:30 PM | Finalist demos, in order. When called, join the [demo call](https://meet.google.com/ajf-fryh-age) and share your screen |
| 5:30 to 5:45 PM | Judges rank the projects and agree the winners |
| 5:45 PM | Winners announced |
| 6:00 PM | Closing announcement and clean up |

The finalists are the submissions with the most judge votes, with at least two from each challenge. Keep
your project running and ready to share either way.

The panel includes the organizer and Illumination Health + Home (IHH) staff. IHH's view of what
would help on day one carries the most weight.

For Challenge 1, judges may hand you a referral packet your tool has not seen and ask you to run it.

## How winners are picked

Each judge scores every finalist from 1 to 5 in three areas. Scores are weighted, averaged across
judges, and the highest totals win. If two teams are close, the judges decide together, with IHH's
view counting most.

| Weight | Area | The question |
|---|---|---|
| **40%** | **Day-one impact** | Is it ready to use, and would it help IHH on day one? |
| **35%** | **Product and idea** | Is it easy to use and to get started with? How creative and useful is the idea, whatever the result? |
| **25%** | **Clarity of the demo** | Are the problem, the user and the outcome obvious? |

A perfect project scores 5 in all three, a weighted total of 5.0.

## What does not place

- Slides without a working demo.
- Features that need IHH's live systems to mean anything.
- Anything built on real patient or client information. That is a disqualification.
