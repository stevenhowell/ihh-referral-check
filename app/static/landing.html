2 · How it works

Every claim points to its page. A person makes the call.

Claude reads each fax page and checks it against IHH's own rules by rule ID. Fetch double-checks every quote before a coordinator sees it.

F

Finds the evidence

Reads every page, turns sideways ones upright, and marks what it can't read instead of guessing.

T

Traces it to the page

Each finding carries the page and the exact words. One click opens the fax with the words boxed.

C

Checks it twice

Code matches every quote to the page text; Claude finds it again on the page image. Misses say "check by hand".

H

Hands it to the coordinator

What rules them out, what needs judgment, what to arrange first. The coordinator decides and exports the record.
