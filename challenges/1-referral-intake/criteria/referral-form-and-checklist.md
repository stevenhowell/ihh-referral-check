# The referral form and checklist

From IHH's Recuperative Care Referral Form (form RC0725-SH). The original PDF is in
[`source/`](source/). The wording below is IHH's.

## What recuperative care is

Recuperative Care with Illumination Health + Home offers short-term medical housing and supportive
services for individuals experiencing homelessness who are ready for hospital discharge but need a
safe place to recover.

## To qualify, a referral must meet all of the following

- Currently unhoused, at risk of being unhoused, or are living in an inadequate environment post
  hospitalization
- Medically and psychiatrically stable condition upon discharge
- The patient is AAOx4 (alert and oriented)
- Able to manage ADL (activities of daily living)
- Able to self-administer medications and manage their medication schedule with staff oversight
- Capable of walking 100 ft. with or without DME and able to transfer independently

## Not eligible if

- Cognitively impaired (i.e., dementia, Alzheimer's)
- Unable to perform activities of daily living
- Unable to transfer, ambulate 100 ft., or use DME independently
- Active infection has not been cleared by a doctor or CID: MRSA, Tuberculosis, C-DIFF
- Non-compliance with detox treatment or a refusal to abstain from substance abuse
- Registered sex offender
- Aggressive or combative behavior directed at staff or other patients

## Referral checklist: what the hospital sends

| ID | Document |
|---|---|
| DOC-1 | Illumination Health + Home Referral Form |
| DOC-2 | Client's Demographic Page |
| DOC-3 | CalOptima Form/LOA/AUTH |
| DOC-4 | H&P (History and Physical) |
| DOC-5 | Current Progress Notes (if needed) |
| DOC-6 | Updated Vitals (last 24 hours) |
| DOC-7 | Medication List |
| DOC-8 | PT/OT or Nursing Notes |
| DOC-9 | Blood or Wound Culture (if needed) |
| DOC-10 | 30-day supply of all medications |

## The service promise

Once the Illumination Health + Home Referral Form has been completed online, emailed or faxed, you
will receive notice of confirmation within two hours. A referral coordinator will review and may
contact the referring hospital for clarification or to request additional information. A decision
regarding approval or denial will be communicated to the referring party within two hours of
receiving the referral. If denied, a brief explanation will be provided.

## What the form asks

**Referring party.** Hospital, department or floor, social worker / case manager / nurse, phone,
email, nursing station, authorized by.

**Patient information.** Name, MRN, date of birth, gender, ethnicity, last four of Social Security
number, insurance, preferred language, housing situation (currently unhoused; 4+ episodes of
homelessness in the past 3 years).

**Hospital admission details.** Chief complaint or admitting diagnosis, admit date, any wounds
(number, location, size and stage), independent with wound care, whether home health will be
ordered, PPD/TB test or chest X-ray with date and result.

**History of health and living situation.** Yes or no for each: O2, self-administers all
medications, continent of bowel and bladder, colostomy or ileostomy, Foley catheter, diabetes,
insulin, diabetes oral medications, active infection, ambulatory, assistive device, hospice, car,
service animal.

**Behavioral and mental health status.** Any limitations or challenges; mental health diagnosis and
type; non-compliant; forgetful; cognitive impairment; registered sex offender; other.

**Length of stay authorized**, in days.

**Illumination staff only.** Date received, reviewed by, approved yes or no, date, authorization
number, reason for denial, admission date and time, exit date, extension request.
